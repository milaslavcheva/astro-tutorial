# Astro Tutorial Notes

## Unit 1

I was surprised by how quickly I could create a working Astro project. I also learned that Astro already gives you a clear structure for where to put pages and components.

## Unit 2

I was surprised that creating new pages and blog posts was quite simple. I also liked that I could use Markdown for the blog posts instead of writing everything in HTML.

## Unit 3

I was surprised by how useful components are. Instead of copying the same navigation and footer into every page, I could create them once and reuse them.

## Unit 4

I liked learning about layouts because it made the pages much cleaner. The same header, footer and other parts can be reused without writing them again every time.

## Unit 5

I was surprised that Astro could automatically find all my blog posts and create pages for things like tags. The RSS feed was also something I had never worked with before.

## Unit 6

I found the interactive parts the most interesting. I liked seeing the greeting change when clicking the button and making the dark mode toggle with JavaScript and Astro.
